# Privacy Policy for Batak Tournament

**Last Updated:** February 10, 2026

## Introduction

Batak Tournament ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Account Information
- **Wallet Address:** If you connect via Solana wallet (Phantom, Seeker, etc.), we store your public wallet address for authentication and rewards distribution
- **Email Address:** If you register via email, we store your email address and encrypted password
- **Username:** Optional display name for leaderboards

### 2. Game Data
- **Game Statistics:** Win/loss records, scores, games played, tournament results
- **Match History:** Records of games played, opponents, and outcomes
- **Achievements:** cNFT rewards earned through tournament wins

### 3. Technical Information
- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system version (for bug fixes and optimization)
- **Connection Data:** IP address, connection timestamps (for anti-cheat and server stability)
- **Error Logs:** Crash reports and error messages (for improving app stability)

### 4. Information We Do NOT Collect
- ❌ Private keys or seed phrases (we never ask for these!)
- ❌ Financial information beyond wallet addresses
- ❌ Personal identification documents
- ❌ Location data
- ❌ Contacts or photos
- ❌ Microphone or camera access

## How We Use Your Information

### Primary Uses
1. **Game Functionality:** Enable multiplayer matchmaking, AI bot matches, and tournament participation
2. **Leaderboards:** Display rankings and statistics (using username or wallet address)
3. **Rewards Distribution:** Send cNFT rewards to your Solana wallet
4. **Account Management:** Authenticate users and maintain game progress
5. **Customer Support:** Respond to inquiries and resolve technical issues

### Secondary Uses
1. **Analytics:** Understand game usage patterns to improve features
2. **Security:** Detect and prevent cheating, abuse, or fraudulent activity
3. **Communication:** Send important updates about the app (opt-in only)

## Data Storage and Security

### Storage
- **Server Location:** Encrypted cloud servers (AWS/Heroku/similar)
- **Local Storage:** Authentication tokens stored securely on your device
- **Blockchain:** Wallet addresses and cNFT ownership recorded on Solana blockchain (public by design)

### Security Measures
- ✅ Encrypted HTTPS/WSS connections
- ✅ Bcrypt password hashing (for email accounts)
- ✅ JWT authentication tokens with expiration
- ✅ Server-side game validation (anti-cheat)
- ✅ No storage of private keys or seed phrases

### Data Retention
- **Active Accounts:** Data retained indefinitely while account is active
- **Inactive Accounts:** Data may be deleted after 2 years of inactivity
- **Deletion Requests:** Honored within 30 days (see Your Rights section)

## Third-Party Services

We integrate with the following third-party services:

### Solana Blockchain
- **Purpose:** Wallet authentication, cNFT minting and distribution
- **Data Shared:** Public wallet addresses, transaction signatures
- **Privacy Policy:** https://solana.com/privacy-policy

### Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter
- **Purpose:** Connect to Phantom, Seeker, and other Solana wallets
- **Data Shared:** Public keys, transaction requests
- **Privacy Policy:** https://docs.solanamobile.com/

### Socket.IO (Real-time Communication)
- **Purpose:** Multiplayer game synchronization
- **Data Shared:** Game state, player actions
- **Privacy Policy:** https://socket.io/docs/v4/

We do NOT share your data with advertisers, analytics companies, or other third parties beyond those listed above.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

### Access
Request a copy of all data we have about you. Contact: privacy@bataktournament.com

### Correction
Update your username, email, or other account information via app settings.

### Deletion
Request complete account deletion. This will:
- Remove your email/password from our servers
- Delete game statistics and match history
- **Not affect** blockchain records (cNFTs in your wallet remain yours)

### Opt-Out
Disconnect wallet or delete account to stop data collection immediately.

### Portability
Export your game statistics and match history in JSON format (contact support).

## Children's Privacy

Batak Tournament is **not intended for children under 13**. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us immediately for deletion.
