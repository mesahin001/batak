Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Batak Tournament is committed to protecting your privacy. We collect only the minimum data needed to operate the game and never sell your personal information to third parties.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Batak Tournament, we may collect:

  • Email address and password — if you register with email/password, your email is stored and your password is stored as a secure hash (bcrypt). We never store plain-text passwords.
  • Wallet address — your Solana public key, used to identify your account and authenticate you in the game. This is a public blockchain address.
  • Username — a display name you choose, associated with your account.
  • Game statistics — wins, losses, bids, tricks won, and other in-game performance data.
  • NFT reward records — mint addresses and metadata for cNFT trophies awarded upon tournament victories.
  • Connection data — timestamps and socket session identifiers used for real-time gameplay; not stored permanently.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and maintain your game session.
  • To calculate and display leaderboard rankings and player statistics.
  • To mint cNFT tournament trophies to your wallet on the Solana blockchain.
  • To operate SKR token staking and prize distribution features.

3. Blockchain Data

Batak Tournament operates on the Solana blockchain. Certain data — including your wallet address, NFT ownership, and on-chain transactions — is recorded publicly on the blockchain by design. This data is immutable and publicly accessible independent of our services. We do not control or limit third-party access to public blockchain data.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your wallet address or game data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. Your data may be disclosed only:

  • As required by applicable law or legal process.
  • To operate essential services (e.g., RPC node providers for Solana transactions — only your public key is shared, never private keys).

5. Data Retention

Game statistics and NFT records are retained for the lifetime of the service. You may request deletion of off-chain data (statistics, game history) by contacting us at the address below. On-chain data cannot be deleted.

6. Security

We never store your private key or seed phrase. Authentication is performed via your wallet's cryptographic signing. Server infrastructure is hosted on Hetzner Cloud with TLS encryption in transit.

7. Children's Privacy

Batak Tournament is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance. Material changes will be announced in-app.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions or data deletion requests, contact us at: info@batakci.xyz
