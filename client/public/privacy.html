Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Batak Tournament mobile application available on the Solana dApp Store and at batakci.xyz. Batak Tournament is committed to protecting your privacy. We collect only the minimum data needed to operate the game and never sell your personal information to third parties.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the Batak Tournament app, we may collect:

  • Wallet address — your Solana public key, used to identify your account and authenticate you in the game. This is a public blockchain address and does not expose your private key.
  • Username — a display name you choose, associated with your account.
  • Email address and password — if you register with email/password, your email is stored and your password is stored as a secure one-way hash (bcrypt). We never store plain-text passwords.
  • Game statistics — wins, losses, bids, tricks won, and other in-game performance data used for leaderboards and player profiles.
  • NFT reward records — mint addresses and metadata for cNFT trophies awarded upon tournament victories, stored to display your trophy collection.
  • Device connection data — timestamps and socket session identifiers used for real-time gameplay; not stored permanently after the session ends.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and maintain your game session across devices.
  • To calculate and display leaderboard rankings and player statistics.
  • To mint cNFT tournament trophies to your Solana wallet upon winning a tournament.
  • To operate SKR token staking and prize distribution features.
  • To improve game stability and fix technical issues.

3. Wallet Address Usage

Your Solana wallet public key is used as your primary account identifier. It is stored in our database to link your game statistics, NFT rewards, and account settings. We do not have access to your private key or seed phrase at any time — authentication is performed cryptographically via your wallet app (e.g., Phantom, Backpack, or any Mobile Wallet Adapter-compatible wallet).

4. Blockchain Transactions

Batak Tournament operates on the Solana blockchain. Certain data — including your wallet address, NFT ownership, SKR token balances, and on-chain transactions — is recorded publicly on the Solana blockchain by design. This data is immutable and publicly accessible independent of our services. We do not control or limit third-party access to public blockchain data. All blockchain transactions are initiated only upon your explicit action within the app.

5. Cookies and Analytics

The Batak Tournament mobile application does not use cookies. The web version at batakci.xyz does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics services. We do not run advertising networks or behavioral tracking on our platform.

6. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate the game:

  • Solana RPC providers — for reading blockchain state and submitting transactions. Only your public wallet address is shared, never private keys.
  • Hetzner Cloud — server hosting. Data is stored on servers located in the EU.
  • Cloudflare — CDN and DDoS protection for the web client. Cloudflare may process connection metadata per their privacy policy.
  • nft.storage / IPFS — for storing NFT metadata associated with tournament trophy rewards.

We do not share your personal data with any advertising networks, data brokers, or marketing platforms.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your wallet address or game data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. Your data may be disclosed only:

  • As required by applicable law or legal process.
  • To operate essential services listed under Third-Party Services above (only your public key, never private keys).

8. Data Retention

Game statistics and NFT records are retained for the lifetime of the service. You may request deletion of off-chain data (statistics, game history, account) by contacting us at the email below. On-chain blockchain data (NFT ownership, transaction history) cannot be deleted as it is permanently recorded on the public blockchain.

9. Security

We never store your private key or seed phrase. Authentication is performed via your wallet's cryptographic signing (Mobile Wallet Adapter protocol). Server infrastructure is hosted on Hetzner Cloud with TLS encryption in transit via Cloudflare. Passwords (where used) are stored as bcrypt hashes.

10. Children's Privacy

Batak Tournament is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

11. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data held by us. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below. We will respond within 30 days.

12. Changes to This Policy
