Batak Tournament

A four-player trick-taking game with bidding, trump suits and tournament rounds.

Two classic modes

Koz Maça (spades are trump, bid only) and İhaleli Batak (bid, then choose trump). Highest score wins.

Fair by design

Every rule runs on our server. Hands are hidden, moves are validated, and nobody can cheat from the client.

Play with anyone

Quick matches, private rooms with a six-digit code, or add bots to fill the table. Reconnect if your signal drops.

Trophies on Solana

Tournament winners can claim compressed NFT trophies. Built for the Solana Mobile ecosystem and the Seeker wallet.

About BatakCI

We are a small team based in Istanbul that loves the card games we grew up with. Our goal is simple: make them easy to play with friends anywhere, on any device, without giving up fairness.

We build everything ourselves, from the game server and matchmaking to the web and Android apps, and we publish our legal terms in plain language.
